BMC INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM
Summary and Proposal for Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation
Date: 13 October 2026
=====================================================================


1. SUMMARY IN ONE PARAGRAPH
---------------------------------------------------------------------
BMC already runs strong transaction systems: SAP S/4HANA for finance
and HR, SAP CRM and the MyBMC portal for citizen services, SAP SRM for
tenders, MyBMC MARG and the 1916 helpline for complaints, the Disaster
Management portal with its rain gauges, AQUA for water billing, HMIS for
hospitals, AutoDCR for building approvals, and many department
dashboards. Each one records its own work well. None of them reads
across the others. The BMC Intelligence Platform is not a replacement
for any of these systems. It is a decision layer that sits on top of
them. It brings their data together by ward, explains what is happening
and why, ranks what needs a decision today, and records who decided
what. It works in English and Marathi.


2. THE GAP IN THE EXISTING SYSTEMS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
We reviewed BMC's public systems and every section of
portal.mcgm.gov.in (checked 11 October 2026). Gaps we found:

  a) Data sits in silos. Property tax is in SAP, complaints in MARG and
     UGMS, rainfall in the DM portal, lake levels in daily press notes,
     road works in the Roads Monitoring System, desilting in the SWD
     dashboard. Nobody sees them together for one ward.

  b) Financial data is mostly PDF. Budgets, annual accounts and audited
     accounts are published as PDFs. They are not available as a
     revenue, expenditure and reserve time series.

  c) Collection efficiency and arrears by ward are not visible in one
     place. There is no ranked recovery list.

  d) Grievances are recorded but not analysed. Pendency, repeat
     complaints and citizens' charter breaches by ward are not
     published or trended.

  e) Monsoon signals are separate. Rain gauges, tide tables, flooding
     spots, pumping stations and desilting progress are on different
     pages. There is no ward-level warning that combines them.

  f) Air quality is spread over four sites (MPCB, CPCB, SAFAR and BMC).

  g) Project pages show no linked physical and financial progress.

  h) Tender lists are HTML. Award values and contractor concentration
     are not analysed.

  i) Officers' dashboards are login-only and specific to each
     department. There is no single picture for the Commissioner.

  j) Decisions are not tracked from evidence to action to outcome.


3. WHAT THE PLATFORM ADDS: INTELLIGENCE OVER THE EXISTING SYSTEMS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform has over 100 screens in 20 areas. The main capabilities:

  COMMAND AND DECISIONS
  - Commissioner Cockpit: what needs attention today, ranked by
    severity and exposure, with the weights shown.
  - Executive Overview and City Intelligence Index: the health of the
    city on one screen.
  - Situation Room and City Command Map: live incidents, alerts and
    the response on a map of the 24 wards.
  - Decision Centre and My Tasks: every decision is carried from
    evidence to a named officer, an action and a measured outcome.
  - Alerts and Escalations: what has passed its standard and who
    holds it now.

  REVENUE, PROPERTY TAX AND FINANCE
  - Revenue Intelligence: each revenue stream against target. Uses
    Budget Estimates 2026-27: property tax ₹7,000 cr, water charges
    ₹2,393 cr, development charges ₹12,050 cr.
  - Ward property-tax targets built from FY26 collections (for
    example K/East ₹719 cr, K/West ₹622 cr).
  - Registry Reconciliation: finds properties missing from the tax
    register by matching it against other registers.
  - Recovery Worklist: arrears ranked by what can actually be
    recovered.
  - Budget Intelligence and Public Money: tracks budget heads while
    there is still time to spend them, and covers capital
    commitments, liabilities and pending state dues.
  - Procurement and Contractor Intelligence: reads each contractor's
    whole book of work, award values and vendor concentration.

  WARDS
  - Ward Intelligence on the real 24 ward boundaries, with Census
    population.
  - Ward League Table, Equity and Allocation, Trajectory,
    Commitments, and Like-for-Like Performance, so wards are compared
    fairly.

  MONSOON AND DISASTER
  - Monsoon Intelligence: heavy rain on a high tide, flooding spots,
    rain gauges, the six BRIMSTOWAD pumping stations (Irla, Haji Ali,
    Love Grove, Cleveland Bunder, Britannia, Gazdarbandh) and
    desilting, all read together, so a cause is read once.
  - Disaster, Fire and Emergency, and Urban Resilience screens.

  WATER, SEWERAGE, STORM WATER AND WASTE
  - Water Intelligence: the seven supply lakes from the Hydraulic
    Engineer's report (8 Oct 2026), supply and losses.
  - Sewerage: the eight wastewater treatment facilities (Colaba,
    Worli, Bandra, Dharavi, Versova, Ghatkopar, Bhandup, Malad) with
    their capacities and upgrade dates.
  - Solid Waste: Kanjurmarg, Deonar, Mulund biomining and the refuse
    transfer stations, with daily intake.

  ROADS, HEALTH, ENVIRONMENT AND MORE
  - Roads: condition, defects and an explainable priority engine for
    repairs.
  - Hospitals: 15 named BMC hospitals (KEM, Sion, Nair, Cooper and
    the peripheral hospitals) with published bed counts; disease
    surveillance for malaria, dengue and leptospirosis.
  - Air quality: all 28 monitoring stations inside BMC limits (MPCB,
    SAFAR and BMC) in one view.
  - Also covered: planning, buildings, encroachment, education,
    housing, welfare, markets, heritage, gardens, coastal areas,
    council and committees, legal, HR and workforce.

  CITIZEN SERVICES
  - Grievance Resolution: why problems keep coming back, and who
    should own them.
  - Notified Services Register: right-to-services deadlines and
    breaches.

  AI, MADE SAFE FOR GOVERNMENT USE
  - Municipal Copilot: officers ask questions in English or Marathi
    and get answers with their sources.
  - AI recommendations, agents and workflows, governed by a model
    registry, evaluation, a prompt registry and AI incident logging.

  TRUST AND EVIDENCE
  - Evidence Registry: 126 published figures from 75 public sources,
    each with its source, date and level of confidence.
  - Every number on screen is marked as published, modelled or
    demonstration.
  - Audit trail, data lineage, role-based access and privacy
    governance.

  STATE BENCHMARKING
  - Compares BMC with other Maharashtra corporations on published
    figures.


4. WHAT IS DIFFERENT, IN SHORT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  Existing systems                     BMC Intelligence Platform
  -----------------------------------  --------------------------------
  Record transactions                  Explains and ranks for decisions
  One department each                  All departments, by ward
  PDFs and separate dashboards         One picture, with time series
  Show what happened                   Shows why, and what to do next
  Login-only, by department            One view for the Commissioner,
                                       with role-based access
  Decisions not tracked                Evidence -> decision -> action
                                       -> outcome, by officer
  English portal, Marathi menus        Full English and Marathi
  Numbers without a source             Every figure with its source


5. EXPECTED BENEFITS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Revenue: higher property-tax and water-charge collection through
    ward targets, a ranked recovery list and properties found missing
    from the register.
  - Monsoon: earlier ward-level warnings by combining rain, tide,
    flooding spots and pumping status.
  - Service: fewer repeat complaints and fewer charter breaches,
    because the cause and the owner are identified.
  - Accountability: every decision recorded against the post that
    took it.
  - Time: officers stop assembling the same reports by hand from
    several systems.
  - Transparency: published figures with sources, ready for the
    General Body and the public.

  These benefits will be measured during the pilot against a
  starting baseline. No numbers are promised in advance.


6. CURRENT STATUS (STATED HONESTLY)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The platform is a working demonstration. Every screen runs.
  - Real public BMC data is already in use for: budget and revenue
    heads, ward property tax, the seven lakes, ward boundaries,
    hospitals and beds, treatment plants, pumping stations, waste
    sites, air-quality stations, flooding spots, rainfall, roads
    budget and disease cases.
  - Operational figures (for example daily occupancy, live complaints
    and plant loads) are modelled and clearly labelled as modelled,
    until BMC data access is given.
  - Ward boundaries come from public survey data (DataMeet). They are
    not BMC's official GIS layer and should be replaced with it.
  - It is NOT BMC's live portal and does not say it is.


7. PROPOSAL: HOW TO DEPLOY IT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  PHASE 1: Pilot (8 to 12 weeks)
  - Scope: property tax and revenue, grievances, and monsoon, in 3 to
    4 pilot wards (for example K/East, K/West, H/West and one ward in
    the island city).
  - BMC provides read-only data: SAP property-tax and water-billing
    extracts, MARG/UGMS complaint exports, DM portal APIs (already
    public), the official ward GIS layer, and the SWD desilting
    status.
  - Output: baseline measures, a ward recovery list, a complaint
    root-cause view and a monsoon ward-risk board, used weekly by the
    pilot ward officers.

  PHASE 2: City-wide (3 to 6 months)
  - All 24 wards and all departments.
  - Scheduled connectors to SAP S/4HANA, SAP SRM, HMIS, AutoDCR,
    Roads Monitoring, AQUA and MyBMC Building ID.
  - Commissioner Cockpit and Decision Centre in daily use.

  PHASE 3: Institutionalise (6 to 12 months)
  - AI copilot and recommendations, with the governance layer in
    place.
  - Training for ward officers and department heads.
  - Hand-over of operations to the BMC IT Department.

  WHAT BMC NEEDS TO PROVIDE
  - A nodal officer and a sponsor at the level of an Additional
    Municipal Commissioner.
  - A data-sharing order for read-only access.
  - The official ward GIS layer.
  - Hosting on BMC infrastructure or a MeitY-empanelled government
    cloud.

  SAFEGUARDS
  - Read-only. The platform never writes to SAP or any source system.
  - Citizen personal data is masked. Role-based access and a full
    audit trail.
  - Data stays in India, on BMC or government cloud infrastructure.


8. NEXT STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  A 45-minute demonstration for the Municipal Commissioner and
  department heads, then agreement on the pilot wards and the
  data-sharing order.
