The platform

The intelligence surfaces behind this portal

What this page shows is the front of it. Behind sign-in the same figures are assembled into the surfaces an officer actually decides from — one for each domain the corporation operates.

  • Commissioner’s cockpitThe whole city on one screen, ordered by what needs a decision today.
  • Situation roomLive incidents, escalations and the control-room picture as it stands.
  • Ward intelligenceEvery one of the Wards scored on service, equity and delivery.
  • Revenue and property taxDemand, collection, arrears and the recovery worklist behind them.
  • Water and sewerageSupply, pressure, non-revenue water and treatment load.
  • Solid wasteCollection routes, lifting, processing and the load on disposal.
  • Monsoon and storm waterRainfall, desilting progress and where the water will stand.
  • Disaster and emergencyPreparedness, response and the city’s standing resilience picture.

Assurance

Built to be answerable

A decision an officer cannot account for is worse than no decision. Four properties hold across every surface behind this portal, and each of them is inspectable from inside the platform.

  • 01

    Sovereign by deployment

    The platform contacts nothing over a network — not a model, not a map tile, not a font. It runs entirely where the corporation runs it.

    Security posture
  • 02

    Answerable by position

    Sign-in is by the post held, not by the person. The post carries the authority and the data scope, and the officer is named against every action taken under it.

    Access and roles
  • 03

    Provenance on every figure

    Each surface states where its numbers came from and which of them are modelled. Nothing is presented as an observation it is not.

    Trust Centre
  • 04

    AI that shows its working

    A recommendation arrives with its inputs, its confidence and the post that is permitted to act on it. An officer decides; the platform never does.

    AI governance
