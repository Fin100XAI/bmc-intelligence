Civic architecture photographs used on the public portal landing page.

These are the seats of the state government under whose Urban Development
Department every municipal corporation in this roster sits. They are used as
ILLUSTRATIVE CIVIC IMAGERY only: none of them is the headquarters of the
corporation the deployment is currently rendered for, and the page never
captions them as such.
