Photographs of Mumbai used by the BMC Intelligence Infrastructure demonstration
===============================================================================

Every photograph in public/cover/ is a real photograph from Wikimedia Commons, published
under the free licence named below. Each was downloaded from upload.wikimedia.org (a
resized rendition of the original; no other changes). They are civic imagery captioned
for what they show; none of them is an official image of the Brihanmumbai Municipal
Corporation.

CC BY / CC BY-SA / FAL licences require attribution: author and licence are shown on the
portal page ("Mumbai in pictures").
